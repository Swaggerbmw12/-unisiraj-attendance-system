==========================================
QUICK DATABASE UPDATE - COPY & PASTE THIS
==========================================

Step 1: Open phpMyAdmin (http://localhost/phpmyadmin)
Step 2: Click on database "unisiraj_attendance"
Step 3: Click "SQL" tab
Step 4: Copy and paste this SQL:

ALTER TABLE courses ADD COLUMN lecturer_name VARCHAR(255) NULL AFTER lecturer_id;

Step 5: Click "Go"
Step 6: Done! Refresh your intake dashboard page

==========================================
THAT'S IT!
==========================================

Now when you add a course with a lecturer name, it will save and display correctly!
