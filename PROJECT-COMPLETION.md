## 💡 Innovation Highlights

### ML-Inspired Risk Detection Algorithm

**Multi-Factor Risk Scoring System:**
```
Risk Score = (100 - Attendance%) × 0.4     // 40% weight
           + Trend_Penalty × 0.3            // 30% weight  
           + (Recent_Absences × 10)         // 30% weight

Score Range: 0-100
Risk Levels: Low (0-29), Medium (30-49), High (50-69), Critical (70-100)
```

**Innovation Value:**
- Proactive student success support
- Early intervention capability
- Data-driven decision making
- Predictive insights without ML framework
- Practical and implementable
